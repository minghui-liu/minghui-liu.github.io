Research Notes That Compound Over Time

July 29, 2026 · 3 min read Preview

A lightweight system for turning daily experiments and questions into durable research context.

The most useful research notes are not transcripts of a day. They preserve decisions: what changed, what happened, and what you now believe.

A small daily record

For each experiment, I try to save the hypothesis, configuration, result, and one sentence of interpretation. The format matters less than keeping these pieces together.

Write for retrieval

Use stable project names and link a new observation to the question that motivated it. Good notes reduce the cost of resuming an idea after weeks away.

Promote what survives

Daily notes are raw material. Recurring lessons should graduate into short summaries that can guide future work—and be corrected when the evidence changes.
